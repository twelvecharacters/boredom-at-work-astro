### What to expect

- **FlexiSpot:** the October sale prices are the Black Friday prices in most years, with the variation coming through bundles (a free desktop upgrade, a monitor arm thrown in) rather than a lower sticker. If the E7 Pro is at $399.99 or less during the sale, that is the buy.
- **Uplift:** Uplift prices rarely move; the promotion lives in coupon codes and free accessories. The fall code ends November 2, so expect a new code for the sale week. Note that Uplift's current frame is the **V3**; our desk guide was written around the V2, which has been replaced.
- **Branch:** Branch usually runs a sitewide percentage off for Black Friday. Fifteen to twenty percent would put the Duo in the mid-$400s.

### When to buy

If the price you see in mid-November matches the table, buy. Waiting for Black Friday itself rarely beats it and risks the size or color you want selling out.

### What to expect

- **Secretlab:** the biggest sale of its year is around Black Friday, typically a cut below the current $579 plus accessory bundles. Secretlab's listed price is already "selling price" versus "non-direct pricing," so measure any deal against $579, not $669.
- **Branch:** sitewide percentage off is the pattern. The Ergonomic Chair at $369 has room to land around $300 to $330 if Branch repeats a 10 to 20 percent cut.
- **Herman Miller:** new Aerons do not drop much, and the brand's own site rarely discounts them. The Black Friday play is a refurbished Aeron from an authorized seller or a dealer promotion. Check the warranty terms and get the size right; a size B fits most people.

### When to buy

Chairs are the item to actually wait for. Unlike desks, the chair brands do hold a bit of discount back for the sale week. Set your target below today's price, and remember that a chair you cannot return is not a deal at any price.

## How to Buy Without Overpaying

1. **Pick the model now**, with our [office chair](/best-office-chairs/) and [standing desk](/best-standing-desks/) guides.
2. **Write down today's price** from the tables above. That is your reference. Strike-through "regular" prices on the sale page are not.
3. **Check the return window** before paying. Thirty days is normal; shorter is a warning sign.
4. **Count the bundle honestly.** A free monitor arm is worth something only if you were going to buy one. See [best monitor arms](/best-monitor-arms/) for what a decent one costs.
5. **Buy when the number beats the table.** Then stop looking. A better deal on Cyber Monday is a possibility, not a reason to skip a good price.
