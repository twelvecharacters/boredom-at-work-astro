> **A note on prices:** this roundup is written before the sale and explains the patterns from past years. It will be updated during the sale weekend with what is actually live. Baseline prices in the linked previews were checked on each brand's website in October 2026.

Cyber Monday is tomorrow, November 30, and it is the last day of a sale that has been running for over a week. It is not a second Black Friday. For most physical products the price is the same as Friday's, some stock is gone, and the only new element is a handful of online-only offers. That makes Monday simple: buy what you researched and did not buy yet, and ignore everything else.

This roundup walks through each category we previewed in October, what usually continues into Monday, and what usually ends.

## Kindle: Prices Usually Hold

Amazon sets one sale price for its own devices and tends to hold it through Cyber Monday. If the Paperwhite or base Kindle hit the range in our [Kindle Black Friday preview](/kindle-black-friday-deals/), that price is most likely still there on Monday.

- **Continues:** device pricing on current Kindle models, Kindle Unlimited promotional offers for new subscribers.
- **Ends or runs out:** bundles with cases and chargers, Kids editions in specific colors.
- **Monday move:** if you have not decided, our [which Kindle should you buy](/which-kindle-should-you-buy/) guide takes five minutes, and the [best Kindle cases](/best-kindle-cases-2026/) are usually still discounted.

## 3D Printers and Filament: Stock Decides

Printer deals from Bambu Lab, Creality, and Elegoo typically run across the whole window, but popular models sell through. Filament bundles are the thing that quietly ends.

- **Continues:** printer pricing while stock lasts, resin and tool bundles.
- **Ends:** multi-spool filament deals, limited-color runs.
- **Monday move:** our [3D printer Black Friday preview](/3d-printer-black-friday-deals/) has the baseline prices. If the printer you wanted is sold out, the [best 3D printers under $500](/best-3d-printers-under-500-vs-diy/) guide lists the alternatives worth buying at regular price.

## Headphones and Earbuds: Last Year's Models Stay Cheap

Sony, Bose, and Apple discounts on current models mostly hold through Monday. The deeper cuts on previous-generation models stay until stock runs out, which can be Monday or weeks later.

- **Continues:** current-model discounts at major retailers.
- **Ends:** retailer-specific doorbusters on older models.
- **Monday move:** check our [headphone and earbud Black Friday preview](/headphone-earbud-black-friday-deals/) for the target prices, and the [best wireless earbuds under $100](/best-wireless-earbuds-under-100/) for the budget tier.

## Standing Desks and Chairs: Codes Often Extend

Furniture is the category most likely to get a Cyber Monday extension. FlexiSpot's fall-sale pricing typically carries through, Uplift usually issues a fresh coupon, and Branch and Secretlab often extend their sitewide offers by a day or two.

- **Continues:** desk pricing, most chair promotions.
- **Ends:** accessory bundles and free-upgrade offers that were tied to Friday.
- **Monday move:** the baseline is in our [standing desk and chair Black Friday preview](/standing-desk-chair-black-friday-deals/). If the chair you chose is below that table, buy it. The broader checklist is in the [home office Black Friday guide](/home-office-black-friday-guide/).

## Software and Subscriptions: Monday Is Their Day

This is the one category where Cyber Monday is genuinely the better day. Budgeting apps, note-taking tools, cloud storage, and online courses lean on Cyber Monday promotions for new subscribers.

- **Monday move:** if you started a trial after reading our [best budgeting apps](/best-budgeting-apps/) guide, Monday is when the first-year discount is most likely to appear. The same goes for AI tools; our [best AI tools for office work](/best-ai-tools-office-work/) guide lists the ones worth paying for.

## The Cyber Monday Checklist

1. **Price history first.** Keepa or CamelCamelCamel for Amazon, our preview tables for everything else. Our [cashback and deal apps](/best-cashback-deal-apps-black-friday/) guide explains the setup.
2. **Portal, then coupon, then card.** Click through Rakuten or TopCashback, let one coupon extension test codes, pay with the card that earns the most.
3. **Only the list.** A Cyber Monday badge on an item you did not plan to buy is the most expensive thing on the internet today.
4. **Check returns.** Sale items sometimes carry shorter return windows. Read it before you pay.
5. **Done means done.** When the list is empty, the sale is over for you, regardless of what the emails say on Tuesday.
