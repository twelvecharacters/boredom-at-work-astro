A home office has two purchases that matter, the chair and the desk, and a long tail of accessories that mostly do not. Black Friday is the week to buy the first two and the week to be disciplined about the rest. This guide gives you the baseline prices so you can tell a real markdown from a badge, and a short list of what to skip.

If you are building from scratch, read our [desk upgrade guide](/desk-upgrade-guide/) first. It sets the order: chair, monitor position, desk, cable management. The sale does not change that order, it only changes the timing.

Two notes. Uplift has moved to the **V3 frame**; if you read our [best standing desks](/best-standing-desks/) guide, the V2 it recommends is the previous generation. And FlexiSpot's "regular" prices are strike-throughs that move with the sale, so compare with the table, not the badge.

**What to skip:** the cheapest no-name frames. Motor quality and wobble at standing height are where the $150 desks fall apart, and Black Friday is full of them. See the [standing desk vs sitting desk](/standing-desk-vs-sitting-desk/) piece if you are still deciding whether you need one at all.

## 2. Office Chairs: Buy the One You Already Chose

Chairs are the biggest health decision in the setup and the item most people get wrong under sale pressure. Pick the model before the sale using our [best office chairs](/best-office-chairs/) guide, then buy it when the price drops.

| Chair | Listed price (Oct 9, 2026) | Regular price | What to expect |
|-------|---------------------------|---------------|----------------|
| Secretlab Titan Evo (Regular, leatherette) | $579 | $669 | Secretlab runs its deepest sale of the year around Black Friday |
| Branch Ergonomic Chair | $369 | $369 | Sitewide percentage off is Branch's usual pattern |
| Herman Miller Aeron | $1,775 to $2,840 | same | Little movement on new units; look at refurbished and dealer promotions |

Secretlab's listed price already reflects a discount from the $669 "non-direct" price. The real question in sale week is whether the bundle offers (accessories thrown in) are worth more to you than a further cut.

**What to skip:** any chair you cannot return. A chair is a 30-day decision, not a 30-second one. Check the return window before you buy, even on sale.

## 3. Monitors: Last Year's Models Fall Hardest

Monitor discounts on Black Friday are real, but they concentrate on previous-generation panels while the current models move a little. That is fine for office work; a 2025 27-inch 1440p IPS panel does the job in 2026. Our [best monitors for working from home](/best-monitors-working-from-home/) guide lists the models worth watching, and the [dual monitor setup guide](/dual-monitor-setup-guide/) covers whether you want two of them or [one ultrawide](/ultrawide-vs-dual-monitors/).

**What to expect:** the steepest cuts on 2025 models from Dell, LG, and Samsung, and bundle pricing when you buy two.

**What to skip:** "gaming" monitors with high refresh rates at a premium if you work in spreadsheets and documents. You are paying for a feature you will not use.

## 4. Monitor Arms, Light Bars, Webcams, Docks

The accessories are where Black Friday turns into clutter. A rule that works: buy only what is already on your list.

- **Monitor arms:** a good arm is a one-time purchase and the sale is a fine time for it. Our [best monitor arms](/best-monitor-arms/) guide covers load ratings, which is the spec that matters.
- **Light bars:** BenQ and Xiaomi models do get discounted. See [best monitor light bars](/best-monitor-light-bars/).
- **Webcams:** the Logitech lineup is reliably on sale. If your laptop camera is from 2022 or earlier, this is the cheap upgrade that improves every call. See [best webcams for working from home](/best-webcams-working-from-home/).
- **Docking stations:** docks rarely see deep cuts, but Thunderbolt models from CalDigit and Anker do get 15 to 25 percent off in past sales. See [best USB-C docking stations](/best-usb-c-docking-stations/).
- **Headphones:** covered separately in our [headphone and earbud Black Friday preview](/headphone-earbud-black-friday-deals/), and the office picks in [best noise-canceling headphones for the office](/best-noise-canceling-headphones-office/).

**What to skip:** no-name arms under $25, bargain webcams with "4K" in the name and a tiny sensor, and accessory bundles. For small, cheap upgrades that actually work, our [best desk upgrades under $100](/best-desk-upgrades-under-100/) list is the better shopping list.

## 5. The Under-$100 Layer

If the chair and desk are done, the sale is also good for the small ergonomic fixes: [desk mats](/best-desk-mats/), an [ergonomic mouse](/best-ergonomic-mice/), a [laptop stand](/best-laptop-stands/), and a [footrest](/best-footrests/). None of these need Black Friday to be affordable, but a few dollars off is a few dollars off. Set a cap, buy what you planned, and stop.

## A Simple Black Friday Plan for Your Office

1. **Chair first.** Decide the model this week. Note today's price from the table above. Buy when it is lower.
2. **Desk second.** Same approach. The fall sale prices are probably the Black Friday prices; a further drop is a bonus, not a requirement.
3. **Monitor if yours is old.** Target last year's model.
4. **Accessories only from your list.** If it was not on the list before the sale, it is not a deal.
5. **Budget the whole thing.** Our [best budgeting apps](/best-budgeting-apps/) guide takes twenty minutes and stops the sale from running you.
