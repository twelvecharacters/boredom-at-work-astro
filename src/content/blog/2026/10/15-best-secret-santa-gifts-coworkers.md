Drew a coworker's name for Secret Santa and have no idea what they like? Good news: the best office gifts under $25 are useful, cheerful, and safe for absolutely anyone. Here are ideas that will not sit forgotten in a drawer, with specific picks and prices where a brand's own store lists them (checked October 9, 2026).

The golden rule: **keep it useful and neutral.** A gift someone can put on their desk or drink is far better than something too personal.

## Desk Gifts That Work for Anyone

- **A small desk plant** (a succulent or a low-maintenance faux plant). It brightens a workspace and needs almost no care. Pair it with a plain ceramic pot and it looks intentional.
- **A quality mug** paired with good coffee, tea, or hot chocolate. Consumables are the safest gift there is: used, enjoyed, gone. A local roaster's bag beats a supermarket brand at the same price.
- **A phone or tablet stand** for propping up a device during calls or lunch videos. Folding aluminum stands run $10 to $20 and fit in a drawer.
- **A cable organizer** or a set of cable clips to tame the desk spaghetti. Our [cable management tips](/cable-management-tips/) show why it is such a relief.
- **A desk mat** if your budget stretches: a reversible PU leather pad is $8 to $15 and makes any desk look tidier. See our [best desk mats](/best-desk-mats/) picks for sizes.

## Stationery and Small Luxuries

- **Field Notes Original Kraft, 3-pack ($12.95).** Pocket notebooks that look good on any desk and get used for lists, meeting notes, and doodles. The Pitch Black version is $14.95 if the recipient's desk is more minimal.
- **MUJI gel ink pens.** A single 0.38 mm or 0.5 mm gel pen is $1.90 to $2.40, and the **10-color set is $18.90**. They write better than the office ballpoints, and a set of colors is a small luxury that costs almost nothing.
- **A quality notebook.** An A5 hardcover from Leuchtturm1917 or Moleskine sits right at the $25 line; check the current price at a retailer. Our [books for productive procrastination](/books-for-productive-procrastination/) piece has ideas if you would rather gift reading than writing.
- **Sticky notes or a desk pad** in a fun design, under $10.
- **A hand cream or lip balm** for dry winter offices. Neutral scents only; unscented is the safe pick.

## Fun (But Still Safe) Gifts

- **A desk toy or fidget** for the stressed-out coworker: a magnetic sculpture, a wooden puzzle, a small kinetic toy.
- **A funny but workplace-appropriate mug.** The test: would you be comfortable if it ended up in a team photo?
- **A snack box** of nice chocolates or a gourmet treat. Check for allergies if you can do it discreetly.
- **A tiny USB gadget** like a mini desk fan or a clip-on light. Our [best tech gifts under $50](/best-tech-gifts-under-50/) list has plenty that dip under $25, and the [tech stocking stuffers](/best-tech-stocking-stuffers/) roundup is all under $25 by design.

## Gifts by Personality

| The coworker who… | Gift |
|-------------------|------|
| Is always writing things down | Field Notes 3-pack or the MUJI pen set |
| Has a plant on every surface | A new small plant, or a nice pot for the one they have |
| Is on calls all day | A phone stand or a cable organizer |
| Runs on coffee | A good mug with a bag from a local roaster |
| You know nothing about | The mug and coffee. It has never failed. |

## What to Avoid

- **Anything too personal:** perfume, clothing, jewelry, or anything size-dependent.
- **Alcohol,** unless you know the person and your workplace is clearly fine with it.
- **Political, religious, or edgy humor** gifts. Secret Santa is not the place.
- **Gift cards for exactly $25.** Technically fine, but it reads as "I did not try." If you go that route, make it a specific place they like.
- **Clutter with no use.** Novelty items that do nothing tend to get tossed in January.

## The Bottom Line

For a Secret Santa gift under $25, aim for **useful and universal**: a Field Notes pack, the MUJI pen set, a desk plant, a nice mug with real coffee or tea, or a phone stand. Keep it neutral, keep it cheerful, and your coworker will actually use it.
