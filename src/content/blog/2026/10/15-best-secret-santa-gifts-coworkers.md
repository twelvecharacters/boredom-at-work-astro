Drew a coworker's name for Secret Santa and have no idea what they like? Good news: the best office gifts under $25 are useful, cheerful, and safe for absolutely anyone. Here are ideas that will not sit forgotten in a drawer.

The golden rule: **keep it useful and neutral.** A gift someone can put on their desk or drink is far better than something too personal.

## The Quick Picks

- **Safest overall:** a small desk plant.
- **Always welcome:** a nice mug with quality coffee or tea.
- **Most practical:** a phone or tablet stand.
- **For the tidy desk:** a cable organizer.

## Desk Gifts That Work for Anyone

- **A small desk plant** (a succulent or a low-maintenance faux plant). It brightens a workspace and needs almost no care.
- **A quality mug** paired with good coffee, tea, or hot chocolate. Consumables are the safest gift there is.
- **A phone or tablet stand** for propping up a device during calls or lunch videos.
- **A cable organizer** or a set of cable clips to tame the desk spaghetti. Our [cable management tips](/cable-management-tips/) show why it is such a relief.
- **A nice desk mat** if your budget stretches, it makes any desk look tidier. See our [best desk mats](/best-desk-mats/) picks.

## Stationery and Small Luxuries

- **A quality pen** feels far nicer than the office ballpoints and is universally useful.
- **A good notebook** for planners, list-makers, and doodlers.
- **Sticky notes or a desk pad** in a fun design.
- **A hand cream or lip balm** for dry winter offices (neutral scents only).

## Fun (But Still Safe) Gifts

- **A desk toy or fidget** for the stressed-out coworker.
- **A funny but workplace-appropriate mug.**
- **A snack box** of nice chocolates or a gourmet treat.
- **A tiny USB gadget** like a mini light or a desk fan.

For more small tech ideas, our [best tech gifts under $50](/best-tech-gifts-under-50/) list has plenty that dip under $25 too.

## What to Avoid

- **Anything too personal:** perfume, clothing, or anything size-dependent.
- **Alcohol,** unless you know the person and your workplace is fine with it.
- **Political, religious, or edgy humor** gifts. Secret Santa is not the place.
- **Clutter with no use.** Novelty items that do nothing tend to get tossed.

## The Bottom Line

For a Secret Santa gift under $25, aim for **useful and universal**: a desk plant, a nice mug with quality coffee or tea, a phone stand, or a good notebook and pen. Keep it neutral, keep it cheerful, and your coworker will actually use it.
