Stockings are where the fun, small gifts go, and tech has plenty of pocket-sized winners under $25. The trick is to pick things people will actually use, not novelty gadgets destined for a drawer. Here are the best tech stocking stuffers for 2026.

(Looking for slightly bigger gifts? Our [best tech gifts under $50](/best-tech-gifts-under-50/) list covers the next tier up.)

## Everyday Useful (You Cannot Go Wrong)

- **A good braided charging cable.** Everyone needs one, and a durable, nicely made cable beats the fraying freebie in the drawer.
- **A slim power bank.** A pocket-sized battery for topping up a phone on the go. One of the most-used gifts on this list.
- **A cable organizer** or a set of magnetic cable clips to tame the desk and bag spaghetti.
- **A USB-C hub or adapter** for anyone with a modern laptop and not enough ports.

## Small but Delightful

- **Budget wireless earbuds.** Decent-sounding earbuds exist well under $25 now, perfect as a backup pair or a first set.
- **A clip-on book light** for the reader in the family. Pairs nicely with our [gifts for book lovers](/best-gifts-for-book-lovers/) picks.
- **A phone grip or ring stand** for one-handed scrolling and propping up videos.
- **A mini LED light** or a tiny clip fan for the desk.

## Clever Little Gadgets

- **A Bluetooth tracker tag** to find keys, a wallet, or a bag.
- **A screen-cleaning kit** for phones, laptops, and glasses.
- **A stylus** for tablet users who like to sketch or take notes.
- **A compact travel adapter** for the frequent flyer.

## For the Desk Worker

- **A wrist rest** or a small mouse pad.
- **A phone stand** for calls and lunchtime videos.
- **A cable clip set** to keep charging cables from sliding off the desk.

If you are shopping specifically for a coworker, our [Secret Santa gifts for coworkers](/best-secret-santa-gifts-coworkers/) guide keeps everything neutral and workplace-safe.

## What to Avoid

- **No-name earbuds and cables** that fail within weeks. A slightly nicer version is worth it.
- **App-and-account gadgets** that need a sign-up before they do anything.
- **Single-use novelties.** Cute for five minutes, then forgotten.

## The Bottom Line

The best tech stocking stuffers under $25 are the small things people reach for every day: **a good cable, a power bank, a cable organizer, or budget earbuds.** Choose useful over gimmicky and every one will get used long after the holidays.
