**A note on prices:** exact 2026 Black Friday prices are announced at the sale. The expectations below are based on past years, with current regular prices as the baseline. We will update this as real deals land.

## Discounts to Expect

Based on past Black Friday sales:

| Category | Regular baseline | Typical Black Friday |

These are informed expectations, not guarantees.

## How to Get the Best Audio Deal

- **Know the regular price.** Audio gets fake "discounts" more than most categories, check the normal price first.
- **Premium gear sees the biggest absolute savings.** If you have wanted AirPods or Sony flagships, this is the time.
- **Budget earbuds get the best percentage cuts.** Great for gifts and backup pairs.
- **Watch the full window.** Deals run from early Black Friday week through Cyber Monday.
