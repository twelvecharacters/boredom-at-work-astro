If a Kindle is on your list, Black Friday is the time to buy. Amazon discounts its own devices aggressively during the sale, and Kindles routinely hit their lowest prices of the year. Here is what to expect for Black Friday 2026, and how to make sure a "deal" is actually a deal.

**A note on prices:** exact 2026 Black Friday prices are not announced until the sale itself. The numbers below are expectations based on past years, with current regular prices as the baseline. We will update this as real deals land.

## Which Kindle to Buy (Decide Now)

The smartest move is to pick your model before the sale so you can act fast. Our [which Kindle should you buy](/which-kindle-should-you-buy/) guide sorts it out, but the short version:

- **Most people:** Kindle Paperwhite (normally $159.99).
- **Budget:** base Kindle (normally $109.99).
- **Color content:** Kindle Colorsoft (normally from $249.99).
- **Note-taking:** Kindle Scribe (normally from $429.99).

## Discounts to Expect

Based on how Kindles have gone on sale in past years:

| Model | Regular price | Typical Black Friday range |
|-------|---------------|----------------------------|
| Kindle (base) | $109.99 | ~$80 to $95 |
| Kindle Paperwhite | $159.99 | ~$110 to $130 |
| Paperwhite Signature | $199.99 | ~$160 to $180 |
| Kindle Colorsoft | from $249.99 | ~$200 to $230 |
| Kindle Kids | $129.99 | ~$95 to $110 |

Treat these as informed expectations, not promises. Amazon sets the actual prices, and they vary year to year.

## How to Avoid Overpaying

- **Know the regular price.** A "deal" that matches the normal price is not a deal. The table above is your reference.
- **Watch the whole sale window.** Deals often start before Black Friday and run through Cyber Monday. Prices can move during the week.
- **Check the configuration.** "With ads" versions are cheaper; make sure you are comparing like for like, and factor in whether you want to pay to remove ads.
- **Bundles can be worth it.** A Kindle bundled with a case and a charger is sometimes better value than the device alone.

## Do Not Forget the Extras

Black Friday is the best time of year to buy a Kindle. **Decide on your model now** using our [which Kindle should you buy](/which-kindle-should-you-buy/) guide, **note the regular price**, and **only pull the trigger when the discount is genuinely below normal.** Do that and you will get the Kindle you actually want at the best price of the year.
