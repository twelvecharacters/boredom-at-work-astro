Thinking about getting into 3D printing, or upgrading? Black Friday is the best time of year to do it. Printers and filament both see their steepest discounts, which makes the barrier to entry lower than ever. Here is what to expect for Black Friday 2026 and how to buy smart.

**A note on prices:** exact 2026 Black Friday prices are not announced until the sale. The figures below are expectations based on past years, with current regular prices as the baseline. We will update this as real deals land.

## First: FDM or Resin?

Decide this before you shop, because it changes which deals matter:

- **FDM (filament):** best for functional parts, organizers, toys, and household fixes. Cleaner and easier for beginners. See our [best 3D printers under $500](/best-3d-printers-under-500-vs-diy/) guide.
- **Resin:** best for fine detail, miniatures, and models, but messier and requires safety gear. See our [best resin 3D printers for beginners](/best-resin-3d-printers-beginners/) guide.

Not sure? Our [FDM vs resin printing](/fdm-vs-resin-printing/) explainer settles it, and [is 3D printing worth it](/is-3d-printing-worth-it/) helps if you are on the fence entirely.

## Discounts to Expect

Based on past Black Friday sales:

| Category | Regular baseline | Typical Black Friday |
|----------|------------------|----------------------|
| Beginner FDM printer | ~$200 to $300 | often well under $200 |
| Anycubic Photon Mono 4 (resin) | ~$169 | meaningful cuts |
| Elegoo Mars 5 Ultra (resin) | ~$269 to $338 | meaningful cuts |
| Filament (per kg) | ~$20 to $25 | multi-packs at yearly lows |

These are informed expectations, not guarantees. Brands set actual prices at the sale.

## How to Buy Smart

- **Buy the printer and filament together.** Filament multi-packs are cheapest during the sale, stock up while you are at it.
- **Do not forget the extras.** For resin, budget for gloves, IPA, and a wash-and-cure setup (these rarely go on sale but are essential). Our [must-have 3D printing accessories](/must-have-3d-printing-accessories/) guide covers the FDM side.
- **Know the regular price.** Use the baselines above so you can spot a real discount versus a fake one.
- **Watch the whole window.** Deals often start early and run through Cyber Monday.

## No Printer, Just Want Prints?

If you decide printing is not for you but you still want custom parts, an on-demand service can print and ship them. Our [Justway review](/justway-review/) covers how that works and what it costs.

## The Bottom Line

Black Friday is the cheapest time of year to start 3D printing. **Decide FDM vs resin first**, use our buyer guides to pick a model, **know the regular price**, and **buy your filament in the same sale.** Do that and you will be printing for less than you thought possible.
