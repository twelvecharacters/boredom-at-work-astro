You do not need to spend $400 on a smartwatch, but the under-$200 field looks different in 2026 than the usual lists suggest. The cheapest new Apple Watch, the **SE 3, starts at $249**. Samsung's **Galaxy Watch FE has disappeared from Samsung's US store**, replaced by the Galaxy Watch8 from $219.99. That leaves the budget tier to Amazfit, Fitbit, and whatever older stock retailers still have.

Here is what you can actually buy under $200 right now, with prices read on each brand's own US store on October 9, 2026. The trick is still the same: match the watch to your phone first, then choose on battery life.

- **Best value overall:** Amazfit Active 2 (Round), from $99.99.
- **Best battery life:** Amazfit Bip 6, $79.99, up to 14 days.
- **Best for the Fitbit ecosystem:** Fitbit Versa 4, $199.95.
- **Best premium feel under $150:** Amazfit Balance, $119.99 (was $199.99).
- **Closest Apple option:** Apple Watch SE 3, $249 (over budget) or a refurbished previous SE.

## 1. Amazfit Active 2, Best Value

The **Amazfit Active 2** is the standout under $200 and it is not close. For $99.99 in the round version you get a bright AMOLED display, built-in GPS, offline maps, heart rate and sleep tracking, and up to 10 days of battery in typical use (Amazfit's figure; heavy use is up to 5 days). It pairs with both iPhone and Android through the Zepp app.

The square version is $149.99 and looks more like an Apple Watch, which is the only reason to pay the extra $50.

**Best for:** anyone who wants the most watch for the money, on either platform.

## 2. Amazfit Bip 6, Best Battery Life

If charging every day drives you mad, the **Amazfit Bip 6** at $79.99 claims up to 14 days of battery, and 26 days in power-saver mode. It is a lighter, simpler watch than the Active 2, but it covers notifications, GPS, and the health basics, and it costs less than a decent pair of earbuds.

**Best for:** people who prioritize battery life and price over screen quality.

## 3. Fitbit Versa 4, Best for the Fitbit Ecosystem

The **Fitbit Versa 4** sits at $199.95 on the Google Store, which makes the cut by five cents. It is the friendliest option here: easy app, strong sleep and activity tracking, built-in GPS, and it works with iPhone and Android. If your family already shares Fitbit stats, this keeps you in the same app.

**Best for:** Fitbit users and people who want approachable health tracking without a learning curve.

## 4. Amazfit Balance, Best Premium Feel Under $150

The **Amazfit Balance** was a $199.99 watch and is currently $119.99 on Amazfit's US store, sold exclusively through Buy with Prime. It is a step up in build and screen from the Active 2, with body composition and readiness metrics. If the price holds, it is the most watch you can get under $150.

**Best for:** people who want a premium-looking watch and do not mind buying through Amazon's checkout.

## 5. Amazfit Active 3 Premium, The Newer Alternative

Amazfit's newer **Active 3 Premium** is $139.99 at the moment, marked down from $169.99. It is the successor design to the Active 2; whether the extra $40 over the Active 2 Round is worth it comes down to the screen and finish, not features you will miss.

## What About Apple and Samsung?

**iPhone users:** Apple's cheapest new watch is the **SE 3 from $249**, which is a fine watch and over this budget. Under $200 you have two real options: a previous-generation Apple Watch SE from Apple's certified refurbished store or a retailer, or a non-Apple watch. Amazfit and Fitbit both pair with an iPhone, with the usual limits (no Apple Pay, no iMessage replies).

**Android users:** the **Galaxy Watch8 starts at $219.99** before trade-in on Samsung's site, so it is close but over. The older **Galaxy Watch FE** is no longer listed by Samsung in the US; if a retailer still has it under $200, it remains a good Android watch with ECG and body composition, just buy knowing it is end-of-line.

**Garmin:** nothing on Garmin's US site currently sits under $200; the Forerunner 70/170 starts at $249.99. Older models like the Venu Sq 2 show up at retailers below $200, and they are worth it for Garmin's Body Battery and sleep metrics if you find one.

## How to Choose

1. **Start with your phone.** iPhone or Android? Amazfit and Fitbit work with both. Apple and Samsung do not cross over.
2. **Decide how much charging you tolerate.** Daily charging is the price of an Apple or Galaxy Watch. Amazfit watches go a week or two.
3. **Match features to your use.** Notifications and steps: anything here. GPS runs: Active 2, Bip 6, or Versa 4. Serious recovery data: a dedicated tracker.
