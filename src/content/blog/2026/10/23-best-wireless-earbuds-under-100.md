You do not need to spend $250 on earbuds anymore. Under $100, you can get active noise canceling, long battery life, and genuinely good sound, features that were premium-only just a couple of years ago. Here are the best wireless earbuds under $100 in 2026.

(Want the premium comparison instead? See our [AirPods vs Galaxy Buds vs Sony](/airpods-vs-galaxy-buds-vs-sony/) breakdown.)

## The Quick Picks

- **Best overall:** Anker Soundcore Liberty 4 NC.
- **Best for workouts:** JLab Epic Air Sport ANC.
- **Best for small ears / tightest budget:** JLab JBuds Mini (around $40).
- **Best for Android:** Samsung Galaxy Buds FE.

## Best Overall: Anker Soundcore Liberty 4 NC

The **Anker Soundcore Liberty 4 NC** is the one to beat under $100. It offers strong adaptive noise canceling with wind reduction, LDAC hi-res audio support, IPX4 water resistance, and around 50 hours of total battery with the case. It is consistently the most-recommended budget ANC pick, and it is easy to see why: it does nearly everything the expensive earbuds do.

**Best for:** most people who want premium features on a budget.

## Best for Workouts: JLab Epic Air Sport ANC

For the gym or running, fit and durability matter more than anything. The **JLab Epic Air Sport ANC** uses secure over-ear hooks and a rugged IP66 rating that shrugs off sweat and dust, so they stay put through a workout.

**Best for:** runners and gym-goers who need a locked-in fit.

## Best for Small Ears / Tightest Budget: JLab JBuds Mini

The **JLab JBuds Mini** (around $40) is ultra-compact, making it a great fit for smaller ears, and it packs a surprising set of features for the price. If you want a cheap, pocketable backup pair or a first set, this is it.

**Best for:** small ears, tight budgets, and backup earbuds.

## Best for Android: Samsung Galaxy Buds FE

If you are on a Samsung or Android phone, the **Samsung Galaxy Buds FE** integrate nicely and have a natural-sounding ambient mode that lets you hold a conversation without pulling them out. A solid, well-rounded budget choice in the Samsung ecosystem.

**Best for:** Samsung and Android users.

## What to Look For Under $100

- **ANC quality:** the best budget ANC (like the Soundcore) genuinely helps on commutes and in offices.
- **Battery life:** aim for 6+ hours per charge and 24+ with the case.
- **Water resistance:** IPX4 or better if you will sweat or get caught in rain.
- **Multipoint:** pairing to two devices at once is increasingly common and very handy.

For a quieter office specifically, our [best noise-canceling headphones for the office](/best-noise-canceling-headphones-office/) guide covers over-ear options too.

## The Bottom Line

The best wireless earbuds under $100 in 2026 are genuinely great: the **Anker Soundcore Liberty 4 NC** leads for most people, the **JLab Epic Air Sport ANC** wins for workouts, the **JLab JBuds Mini** is the budget and small-ear pick, and the **Samsung Galaxy Buds FE** suits Android users. You lose surprisingly little by skipping the premium tier.
