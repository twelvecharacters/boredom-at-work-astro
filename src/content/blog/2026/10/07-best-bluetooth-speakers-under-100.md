A good portable Bluetooth speaker does not need to cost a fortune. Under $100, you can get room-filling sound, waterproofing, and battery life that lasts all day. Here are the best Bluetooth speakers under $100 in 2026.

## The Quick Picks

- **Best overall:** JBL Flip 6 (around $90).
- **Best battery life:** Anker Soundcore 2 (around 24 hours).
- **Best value:** Tribit StormBox Flow (around $60).
- **Premium pick on sale:** Bose SoundLink Flex (around $100 when discounted).

## Best Overall: JBL Flip 6

The **JBL Flip 6** (around $90) remains the king of sub-$100 portable sound. It delivers JBL's signature punchy, balanced audio, carries an IP67 rating (dust-tight and waterproof), and lasts about 12 hours. It is the safe recommendation for almost anyone.

**Best for:** most people who want great sound and durability in one package.

*(There is also a newer JBL Flip 7 at around $105, just over the $100 line, with upgraded durability and connection options if you can stretch the budget.)*

## Best Battery Life: Anker Soundcore 2

The **Anker Soundcore 2** is the budget champion for endurance, with around 24 hours of playback and solid water resistance for the price. If you want a speaker you rarely have to charge, this is it.

**Best for:** all-day outdoor use and budget buyers.

## Best Value: Tribit StormBox Flow

The **Tribit StormBox Flow** (around $60) punches well above its price: roughly 30 hours of battery, IP67 waterproofing, and sound quality that rivals speakers costing twice as much. A brilliant pick if you want maximum value.

**Best for:** value seekers who still want great sound and long battery.

## Premium Pick on Sale: Bose SoundLink Flex

The **Bose SoundLink Flex (2nd Gen)** normally runs a bit above $100, but it frequently dips to around $100 or less on sale. When it does, it is the premium pick: excellent sound for its size, rugged waterproof build, and about 12 hours of battery.

**Best for:** buyers who want Bose sound and can catch a deal.

## What to Look For Under $100

- **Waterproofing:** IP67 means you can take it to the pool, beach, or shower without worry.
- **Battery life:** 12 hours is plenty for most; 24+ is great for trips.
- **Size vs sound:** bigger speakers sound fuller but are less pocketable. Match it to how you will carry it.
- **Pairing extras:** many can pair two speakers for stereo, handy for a bigger space.

## The Bottom Line

Under $100, the **JBL Flip 6** is the best all-rounder, the **Anker Soundcore 2** and **Tribit StormBox Flow** win on battery and value, and the **Bose SoundLink Flex** is the premium choice when it goes on sale. Any of them will sound far better than your phone and follow you anywhere.
